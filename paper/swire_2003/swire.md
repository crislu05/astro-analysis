1. photometry 

measurment of how much an astronomical object emits. In fits, each pixel holds a count value and photometry turns the counts belonging to a star / galaxty into a calibrated brightness 

1.1 basic steps 

- source detection and bakground estimation 

- sum the flux inside an aperture and convert to a magnitude 

- estimate the uncertainty 

1,2 choices and impact 

- aperture size: a small aperture misses extended galaxy light, big aperture adds noise;

- bacround subraction: errors bias faint soruces
